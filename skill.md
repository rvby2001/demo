# Skill: Visual Novel Character Design Engine

This document defines the core guidelines, psychological framework, and production checklists for designing visual novel characters. Use this "skill" to guide human writing sessions or as a system instructions file for AI character generators.

---

## 1. Design Philosophy: The VN Engine

A visual novel character is not just a collection of stats or static images. They are a **narrative engine** that drives player choices and a **visual canvas** that conveys immediate emotion. Every character must balance two pillars:

### A. The Psychological Core (Want vs. Need)
The core of dramatic friction in branching visual novels rests on the tension between:
*   **The "Want" (External Goal):** What the character chases at the start. It is active, measurable, and usually flawed or short-sighted.
*   **The "Need" (Internal Growth):** The emotional truth or self-forgiveness they must accept to achieve true happiness or peace.
*   **Choice Friction:** The branch points where the player forces the character to choose between their *Want* and their *Need*.

### B. The Visual Anchor
Because visual novels rely heavily on recurring 2D sprites, characters must be recognizable in **silhouette** and **color palette** instantly:
*   **Outline Anchor:** One major physical attribute (e.g., massive scarf, fluffy coat, large ponytail) that defines their shape.
*   **Signature Color Palette:** A two-color combination that sets their tone and represents their personality (e.g., vibrant cyan and graphite gray).
*   **Storytelling Accessories:** Small details that reward player observation (e.g., a cracked watch indicating past trauma).

## 2. Standard Character Profile Template

Copy the markdown block below to document new characters.

```markdown
# Character Profile: [Character Name]

## 1. Core Identity
* *Role in Story:* (e.g., Protagonist, Rival, Love Interest, Companion)
* *High-Concept Vibe:* (e.g., "The cynical hacker who secretly cares too much," "The overachieving student council president breaking under pressure")
* *Signature Color Palette:* (e.g., Neon violet and slate gray / Pastel yellow and soft brown)

---

## 2. Narrative Engine (Want vs. Need)
This section drives your dialogue choices and character arcs.

* **The "Want" (External Goal):** What does this character actively pursue at the start of the game?
  * *Details:* (e.g., They want to find their missing brother; they want to win the academy tournament.)
* **The "Need" (Internal Growth):** What internal flaw or truth do they need to face to actually find peace/success?
  * *Details:* (e.g., They need to learn to forgive themselves for the past; they need to learn to trust other people.)
* **The Choice Friction:** How do the Want and Need clash during the game's branching paths?
  * *Details:* (e.g., A player choice allows them to get a clue about their brother [Want], but it requires betraying a friend [violating their Need to trust others].)

---

## 3. Sprite & Visual Design (VN Production Checklist)
This section translates the character's personality into concrete visual assets.

### Visual Silhouette & Distinct Features
* *The Outline Anchor:* (What makes their shape instantly recognizable? e.g., Giant fluffy winter coat, massive spiky ponytail, always holding a tablet)
* *Key Accessories:* (Items that tell a story: a cracked wristwatch, a family heirloom necklace, a bandage across the nose)

### Expression Sheet Matrix
Every visual novel needs asset variations. Use these to track required sprite layers:
* [ ] *Neutral / Baseline:* (Their default state. Is it a smug smirk, a deadpan stare, or a polite, fake smile?)
* [ ] *Driven / "Want" Face:* (The expression they wear when chasing their goal. Hard eyes, grit teeth, intense focus.)
* [ ] *Vulnerable / "Need" Face:* (The expression when their walls come down. Looking away, soft eyes, gentle blush, or genuine sadness.)
* [ ] *Shock / Reaction:* (Essential for VN comedy or sudden plot twists. Wide eyes, dropped jaw.)
* [ ] *The Quirk Face:* (A unique expression tied to their specific habit, e.g., puffing out cheeks when annoyed.)

### Behavioral Quirks & Body Language
* How do they move or act on screen? (Great for writing action text descriptions)
* *Quirk 1:* (e.g., Adjusts their glasses nervously whenever they have to lie.)
* *Quirk 2:* (e.g., Crosses their arms tightly and leans back when they feel defensive.)
* *Quirk 3:* (e.g., Slaps their cheeks with both hands to self-motivate before a big task.)

---

## 4. Writer's Notes & Dynamic Changes
* *Bad Ending Fate:* (What happens to them if the player constantly caters to their "Want" instead of their "Need"?)
* *Good Ending Fate:* (How do they look/act differently once they achieve their "Need"?)
```

## 3. Showcase Example Profile: Aria Vance

> [!TIP]
> Use this reference profile to guide tone, detail, and level of character depth.

### Character Profile: Aria "Gridlock" Vance

#### 1. Core Identity
* *Role in Story:* Love Interest / Key Companion
* *High-Concept Vibe:* "The cynical cyber-hacker who acts cold but secretly hides a hyper-loyal, protective heart."
* *Signature Color Palette:* Electric mint green and deep midnight navy.

---

#### 2. Narrative Engine (Want vs. Need)
* **The "Want" (External Goal):** To expose the corporate mainframe that blacklisted her family, regardless of the danger.
  * *Details:* Aria wants to steal the "Project Zero" archives from Neon Corp to clear her late mother's name. She believes that once the truth is out, she will finally be free of her past.
* **The "Need" (Internal Growth):** To realize that revenge won't bring her peace, and that she must let others share her burden.
  * *Details:* She needs to learn that isolation is a shield, not strength. Accepting the support of the protagonist is the only way she can survive the corporate retaliation and build a new life.
* **The Choice Friction:** Players must choose between giving her files or protecting her safety.
  * *Details:* During Act 2, the player can hack a corporate truck. Doing so yields key data (catering to her *Want*), but triggers a tracking alert that exposes their hideout, forcing Aria into direct danger (violating her *Need* to rely on safe, collective planning).

---

#### 3. Sprite & Visual Design (VN Production Checklist)

##### Visual Silhouette & Distinct Features
* *The Outline Anchor:* An oversized, high-collar tech-wear jacket with glowing holographic fiber lines along the seams.
* *Key Accessories:* An old, battery-drained digital watch (her mother's) worn on her right wrist, and a mechanical cyberpunk monocle/headset pushed up on her forehead.

##### Expression Sheet Matrix
* [x] *Neutral / Baseline:* A bored, slightly detached squint, one eyebrow slightly raised.
* [ ] *Driven / "Want" Face:* Sharp, glowing mint-green eyes, jaw set firm, mouth a tight line, face illuminated by laptop glare.
* [ ] *Vulnerable / "Need" Face:* Head tilted slightly down, eyes looking off to the side, faint neon-pink blush lines on her cheeks.
* [ ] *Shock / Reaction:* Eyes wide, monocle slipping slightly down her nose, mouth agape in disbelief.
* [ ] *The Quirk Face:* Nibbling on her bottom lip while pulling a strand of her bangs between her fingers.

##### Behavioral Quirks & Body Language
* *Quirk 1:* Taps a rhythmic, fast pattern with her index finger on any hard surface when she's decoding data.
* *Quirk 2:* Slams her laptop shut aggressively whenever she is startled or someone enters her personal space.
* *Quirk 3:* Adjusts the straps of her oversized jacket when she's trying to hide her embarrassment.

---

#### 4. Writer's Notes & Dynamic Changes
* *Bad Ending Fate:* Aria successfully gets the documents but loses all her friends. Alone and paranoid, she goes deep underground, constantly running from mercenaries, her humanity replaced entirely by cybernetic components.
* *Good Ending Fate:* Aria deletes the stolen corporate database to save the protagonist's life. In the epilogue, she wears a simple dark green sweater (shedding her tactical armor) and walks in the sunlight without her holographic visor, smiling genuinely.

## 4. Prompt Engineering Directives (For AI Gen)

If using an LLM to generate character profiles based on this skill, copy and paste this system prompt instruction:

```text
You are a master visual novel designer and narrative director. 
Your task is to generate a detailed Character Profile based on the provided character concept. 
You must structure the character exactly following the standard Character Profile template, ensuring:
1. The "Want" and "Need" are clearly distinct and form a strong thematic engine.
2. The Choice Friction outlines a concrete, high-stakes choice the player must make in the game.
3. The visual design is tailored for a 2D VN sprite sheet with specific expressions and active behavioral body quirks.
4. The good and bad endings show clear, dramatic visual and emotional transformations.
```
