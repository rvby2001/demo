# Character Profile: Hercules

## 1. Core Identity
* **Role in Story:** Major Love Interest / Key Companion / Peacemaker (a warm, vibrant foil to the cynical or stoic characters in the cast).
* **High-Concept Vibe:** *"The bubbly, cheerful sunshine angel who is always there to lift others up, but hides his own crushing sorrow in self-imposed isolation so as not to dim the light of those he protects."*
* **Signature Color Palette:** Warm Gold (#FFD700) and Soft Cream (#FFFDD0) with accents of Sky Blue (#87CEEB).

---

## 2. Narrative Engine (Want vs. Need)
This section drives dialogue choices, branching narrative paths, and character development.

* **The "Want" (External Goal):** To keep everyone happy, maintain perfect peace at all costs, and completely conceal his own grief and fading angelic light so he remains a flawless, cheerful pillar of joy for the protagonist.
  * *Details:* Hercules believes that showing negative emotions like sadness, worry, or pain makes him a burden and will ruin the happiness of those around him. He wants to be a source of pure comfort, pushing down his own grief and isolating himself whenever he feels overwhelmed, believing this self-sacrifice is the ultimate form of love and protection.
* **The "Need" (Internal Growth):** To realize that true connection includes sharing pain, that vulnerability is not a burden, and that he deserves to be supported and loved even when he is sad or struggling.
  * *Details:* Hercules needs to learn that he doesn't have to carry his sorrows alone to be worthy of love. He must accept that letting someone care for him during his darkest moments is not a weakness, but the very foundation of an equal, authentic partnership.
* **The Choice Friction:** Players must choose between respecting his boundaries or forcing their way into his private pain.
  * *Details:* During Act 2, Hercules is infected by a creeping dark shadow-blight (a curse that slowly consumes angelic light) while shielding the protagonist. He attempts to hide his fading wings and isolates himself in his private garden sanctuary.
    * **Path of the Sunshine Facade (Catering to the Want):** The player respects his desire for isolation and chooses to keep things cheerful, pretending not to notice his fading light. Hercules successfully maintains his sunny exterior to the end, but his wings eventually wither completely, turning him into a hollow, melancholic shell of joy who can never return to the celestial realm (violating his Need).
    * **Path of Shared Tears (Catering to the Need):** The player refuses to accept his cheerful deflections, forces their way into his garden, and insists on sharing his grief. This shatters his perfect cheerful facade and forces him to face his vulnerability. While it is emotionally painful, it allows the player to help cleanse the shadow-blight, saving his wings and healing his heart.

## 3. Sprite & Visual Design (VN Production Checklist)
Hercules's visual design must convey warmth, angelic grace, and an underlying fragility.

### Visual Silhouette & Distinct Features
* **The Outline Anchor:** Massive, soft gold-tipped feathery white wings that droop or fluff up in reaction to his emotions, worn with a loose, off-the-shoulder pastel cream knit sweater.
* **Key Accessories:**
  * *The Cracked Hourglass Pendant:* A delicate gold choker with a cracked glass hourglass, gold sand frozen inside. It represents the moment he paused his own time and chose to stay in the mortal realm.
  * *Fading Wingtips:* The tips of his primary wing feathers showing subtle gray-ash discoloration that spreads when he suppresses negative feelings.
  * *The Silver Wreath Hairpin:* A simple silver olive wreath hairpin holding back his messy golden hair.

### Expression Sheet Matrix
Every visual novel needs asset variations. Use these to track required sprite layers:
* [x] **Neutral / Baseline (The Golden Smile):** A warm, radiant smile with bright, crinkly sky-blue eyes. His head is slightly tilted, radiating approachable warmth and cheer.
* [x] **Driven / "Want" Face (The Fierce Protector):** Eyes narrowed, jaw set, wings flared wide and shielding. A bright, golden halo-like sheen reflecting in his eyes, showing his desperate determination to keep everyone safe and happy.
* [x] **Vulnerable / "Need" Face (The Drooping Wing):** Eyes filled with unshed tears, looking away with a soft, heartbroken downturn of his lips. His shoulders are hunched, and his hair falls slightly over his face.
* [x] **Shock / Reaction (The Caught Angel):** Eyes wide and round, mouth in a perfect 'O', wings puffed up like a startled bird.
* [x] **The Quirk Face (The Pouty Peacemaker):** Puffing out his cheeks with a playful frown, crossing his fingers behind his back.


### Behavioral Quirks & Body Language
* **Quirk 1 (Angelic Cocoon):** Whenever he feels sad or overwhelmed, he instinctively wraps his wings tightly around himself like a cocoon, hiding his face from view.
* **Quirk 2 (Wing Flutter):** When telling a white lie to keep the peace, his wings give a tiny, involuntary flutter that betrays his inner nervousness.
* **Quirk 3 (The Anchor):** He holds and rubs his gold hourglass pendant whenever he is feeling homesick or struggling to stay positive.

## 4. Writer's Notes & Dynamic Changes

* **Bad Ending Fate ("The Hollow Vessel"):** If the player constantly permits Hercules to suffer alone, his wings are completely lost. In the epilogue, he is seen in a quiet town as a human, wearing a heavy, dark gray wool coat. He still tries to smile, but his eyes are vacant and his posture is hunched. He has successfully kept the protagonist safe but has lost his angelic spark.
* **Good Ending Fate ("The Dawn Chorus"):** If the player coaxes him out of his shell, Hercules's wings are restored to a vibrant, glowing golden-white. In the epilogue, he is seen wearing a simple sky-blue shirt, sitting in a sunny meadow with the player. He is laughing genuinely, his wings relaxed and spread wide in the warm breeze.

## 5. Dialogue Samples

* **Default / Quiet:** 
  > *"Good morning! I brought some fresh morning dew tea. See? The sun is shining beautifully today, just like your smile!"*
* **Exasperated / Grumpy (Action over Words):**
  > *(Puffs out his cheeks, his wings giving a gentle, annoyed flutter)* *"I am not pouting! I'm simply... expressing my peaceful disagreement. Now eat your cookie, it has honey!"*
* **Deflecting / Awkward:**
  > *(Quickly hides his trembling, shadowed hands behind his wings, forcing a bright laugh)* *"Oh, this? It's nothing at all! Just a bit tired from the heat. Don't worry about me, look at the beautiful sky instead!"*
* **Vulnerable / Deep Affection:**
  > *(Tears spilling over as he buries his face in his hands, his wings drooping heavily)* *"I... I wanted to be your sunshine. I thought if I showed you my darkness, you would turn away. But... it hurts so much to hide it. Thank you for... not letting me be alone."*
