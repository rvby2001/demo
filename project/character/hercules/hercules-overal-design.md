# Master Visual Reference Sheet: Hercules

This document merges the narrative core of the character-design profile for **Hercules** with standard visual prompt engineering blocks to create a single production-ready master reference sheet for visual novel artists and image generators.

---

## 1. Core Identity & Narrative Engine

### Narrative Core
* **Role in Story:** Major Love Interest / Key Companion / Peacemaker (a warm, vibrant foil to the cynical or stoic characters in the cast).
* **High-Concept Vibe:** *"The bubbly, cheerful sunshine angel who is always there to lift others up, but hides his own crushing sorrow in self-imposed isolation so as not to dim the light of those he protects."*
* **Signature Color Palette:**
  * **Warm Gold (#FFD700):** Reflects his angelic light, golden-tipped wings, and sunny disposition.
  * **Soft Cream (#FFFDD0):** Grounded, warm, soft; represented by his loose knit sweater.
  * **Sky Blue (#87CEEB) [Accent]:** Representing his eyes and connection to the sky/celestial realm.

### The Want vs. Need Friction
* **The "Want" (External Goal):** To keep everyone happy, maintain perfect peace at all costs, and completely conceal his own grief and fading angelic light so he remains a flawless, cheerful pillar of joy for the protagonist.
  * *Details:* Hercules believes that showing negative emotions makes him a burden. He pushes down his grief and isolates himself whenever he feels overwhelmed, thinking this keeps others safe and happy.
* **The "Need" (Internal Growth):** To realize that true connection includes sharing pain, that vulnerability is not a burden, and that he deserves to be supported and loved even when he is sad or struggling.
  * *Details:* He must learn that letting someone care for him is not a weakness, and that true relationships thrive on shared honesty rather than forced facades.
* **The Choice Friction:**
  During Act 2, Hercules is infected by a creeping dark shadow-blight (a curse that slowly consumes angelic light) while shielding the protagonist. He attempts to hide his fading wings and isolates himself in his private garden sanctuary.
  * **Path of the Sunshine Facade (Catering to the Want):** The player respects his isolation to keep things cheerful. Hercules successfully maintains his sunny exterior, but his wings eventually wither completely, turning him into a hollow, melancholic shell of joy.
  * **Path of Shared Tears (Catering to the Need):** The player refuses to accept his cheerful deflections, forces their way in, and insists on sharing his grief. This shatters his perfect cheerful facade but allows the player to help cleanse the blight, saving his wings and healing his heart.

## 2. Sprite & Visual Design System

### Visual Silhouette & Distinct Features
* **The Outline Anchor:** Massive, soft gold-tipped feathery white wings that droop or fluff up in reaction to his emotions, worn with a loose, off-the-shoulder pastel cream knit sweater.
* **Key Accessories:**
  * *The Cracked Hourglass Pendant:* A delicate gold choker with a cracked glass hourglass, gold sand frozen inside. It represents the moment he paused his own time and chose to stay in the mortal realm.
  * *Fading Wingtips:* The tips of his primary wing feathers showing subtle gray-ash discoloration that spreads when he suppresses negative feelings.
  * *The Silver Wreath Hairpin:* A simple silver olive wreath hairpin holding back his messy golden-blonde hair.

### Behavioral Quirks & Body Language
* **Quirk 1 (Angelic Cocoon):** Whenever he feels sad or overwhelmed, he instinctively wraps his wings tightly around himself like a cocoon, hiding his face from view.
* **Quirk 2 (Wing Flutter):** When telling a white lie to keep the peace, his wings give a tiny, involuntary flutter that betrays his inner nervousness.
* **Quirk 3 (The Anchor):** He holds and rubs his gold hourglass pendant whenever he is feeling homesick or struggling to stay positive.

## 3. The Prompt Anatomy Formula

To generate a consistent visual novel sprite, compile the three modular blocks using the following syntax:

```text
[STYLE CANVAS] [CHARACTER IDENTITY ANCHOR], [EXPRESSION & POSE LAYER], [PRODUCTION SUFFIX]
```

### Module A: The Static Style Canvas
> *"Visual novel sprite, high-end 2D anime style, clean crisp line art, beautiful soft cell-shading, vibrant color grading, warm glowing light,"*

### Module B: The Static Character Identity Anchor
> *"...a beautifully handsome 20-year-old male angel companion, soft elegant jawline, messy fluffy golden-blonde hair, bright expressive sky-blue eyes, wearing a loose off-the-shoulder pastel cream knit sweater, a pair of massive soft gold-tipped feathery white wings arching from his back, a delicate gold choker with a cracked glass hourglass pendant around his neck, a simple silver olive wreath hairpin in his hair..."*

### Module C: The Static Production Suffix
> *"...waist-up portrait, facing viewer, centered composition, isolated on a solid flat medium-gray background, clean edges, studio light, professional illustration, masterpiece --no 3d, photorealism, background elements, patterns, drop shadows"*

## 5. Writer's Notes & Dynamic Ending Transformations

* **Bad Ending Fate ("The Hollow Vessel"):**
  If the player constantly permits Hercules to suffer alone, his wings are completely lost. In the epilogue, he is seen in a quiet town as a human, wearing a heavy, dark gray wool coat. He still tries to smile, but his eyes are vacant and his posture is hunched. He has successfully kept the protagonist safe but has lost his angelic spark.
* **Good Ending Fate ("The Dawn Chorus"):**
  If the player coaxes him out of his shell, Hercules's wings are restored to a vibrant, glowing golden-white. In the epilogue, he is seen wearing a simple sky-blue shirt, sitting in a sunny meadow with the player. He is laughing genuinely, his wings relaxed and spread wide in the warm breeze.

## 6. Dialogue Samples

* **Default / Quiet:**
  > *"Good morning! I brought some fresh morning dew tea. See? The sun is shining beautifully today, just like your smile!"*
* **Exasperated / Grumpy (Action over Words):**
  > *(Puffs out his cheeks, his wings giving a gentle, annoyed flutter)* *"I am not pouting! I'm simply... expressing my peaceful disagreement. Now eat your cookie, it has honey!"*
* **Deflecting / Awkward:**
  > *(Quickly hides his trembling, shadowed hands behind his wings, forcing a bright laugh)* *"Oh, this? It's nothing at all! Just a bit tired from the heat. Don't worry about me, look at the beautiful sky instead!"*
* **Vulnerable / Deep Affection:**
  > *(Tears spilling over as he buries his face in his hands, his wings drooping heavily)* *"I... I wanted to be your sunshine. I thought if I showed you my darkness, you would turn away. But... it hurts so much to hide it. Thank you for... not letting me be alone."*
