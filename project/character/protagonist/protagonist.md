# Character Profile: Protagonist (Player Avatar)

## 1. Core Identity
* **Role in Story:** Female Protagonist / Player Avatar (POV style).
* **High-Concept Vibe:** *"The ultimate blank-slate protagonist: an adaptable, observant newcomer whose core identity, beliefs, and emotional connections are defined entirely by player choices."*
* **Signature Color Palette:** Soft Beige (#F5F5DC) and Charcoal (#36454F) with accent of Muted Ivory (#FDF5E6) (neutral tones that visually anchor the screen and allow the love interests' palettes to pop).

---

## 2. Narrative Engine (Want vs. Need)
This section drives dialogue choices, branching narrative paths, and character development.

* **The "Want" (External Goal):** To safely navigate her new supernatural surroundings and survive conflicts between powerful factions while keeping her options open.
  * *Details:* Placed suddenly in a world of vampires and hunters, she wants to stay alive, keep the peace, and avoid getting caught in the crossfire. She acts as a passive observer, trying to remain neutral and agreeable to everyone to minimize danger.
* **The "Need" (Internal Growth):** To step out of passive observation, take a definitive stand, and commit to her choices and companions rather than trying to please everyone or remain an invisible bystander.
  * *Details:* She needs to understand that absolute neutrality is an illusion that leads to tragedy. True safety and meaningful connections require taking a side, asserting her own voice, and being willing to face conflict for the sake of those she loves.
* **The Choice Friction:** Represented by the main branching choice pathways of the game where she must make a definitive choice between Silas's sentinel path, Dorian's coven path, or Hercules's celestial path.
  * *Details:* Trying to remain neutral (choosing the "I don't know" or passive options) results in the tragedy of her being a mere bystander as they all fall.
    * **Path of the Bystander (Catering to the Want):** She makes passive, indecisive choices to remain completely neutral. Factions destroy each other, and she is left wandering alone in a ruined city, her journal completely blank, unable to rewrite the history she failed to shape.
    * **Path of the Author (Catering to the Need):** She takes a stand and commits to a path. She is seen standing confidently alongside her chosen companion, her hood completely down, exposing her face to the sunlight, writing the final, hopeful words in her journal with a genuine smile.

---

## 3. Sprite & Visual Design (VN Production Checklist)
Because it is a POV-style game, the protagonist's sprite is rarely shown on screen. However, she is designed as a classic self-insert for promotional art, key CGs, or avatar icons.

### Visual Silhouette & Distinct Features
* **The Outline Anchor:** A soft, neutral cream trench coat with an oversized hood that is often pulled up, partially casting a shadow over her eyes. This allows players of all hair styles to easily insert themselves into her role.
* **Key Accessories:**
  * *The Blank Leather Journal:* A simple, unadorned leather sketchbook where she writes her observations of the world, serving as the in-game diary/menu interface.

### Expression Sheet Matrix
Every visual novel needs asset variations. Use these to track required sprite layers:
* [x] **Neutral / Baseline (The Observer):** Calm, attentive face, eyes partially obscured by soft bangs, representing an active listener.
* [x] **Driven / "Want" Face (The Decisive Path):** Jaw set, hood pushed back slightly to reveal focused eyes, determined to protect her chosen path.
* [x] **Vulnerable / "Need" Face (Open Pages):** Head tilted down, hands clutching her leather journal to her chest, expressing emotional openness.
* [x] **Shock / Reaction (Caught Unawares):** Eyes slightly widened, looking back over her shoulder in surprise.
* [x] **The Quirk Face (The Scribbler):** Holding a pencil to her lip, looking thoughtful as she decides what to write in her journal.


### Behavioral Quirks & Body Language
* **Quirk 1 (Observation Mode):** Whenever she feels overwhelmed in a group conversation, she silently opens her leather journal and starts writing notes or sketching, shifting attention away from herself.
* **Quirk 2 (The Hood Shield):** She pulls the edges of her oversized hood forward when she is embarrassed or trying to hide her reactions from others.
* **Quirk 3 (Pencil Tap):** She taps her pencil rhythmically against her chin when faced with an important decision, a physical cue for player choice prompts.

## 4. Writer's Notes & Dynamic Changes

* **Bad Ending Fate ("The Bystander"):** She remains completely passive, failing to make a choice. Factions destroy each other, and she is left wandering alone in a ruined city, her journal completely blank, unable to rewrite the history she failed to shape.
* **Good Ending Fate ("The Author of Fate"):** Once she achieves her "Need" and commits to a path, she is seen standing confidently alongside her chosen companion. Her hood is completely down, exposing her face to the sunlight, and she writes the final, hopeful words in her journal with a genuine smile.

---

## 5. Dialogue Samples

* **Generic / Observation:** 
  > *"I'm just... trying to understand how this world works. Tell me more about what you do."*
* **Passive / Neutral (Default POV):** 
  > *(Quietly pulls her hood down a bit, opening her journal)* *"I don't want to get between you two. Let's just find a way that keeps everyone safe."*
* **Assertive / Taking a Stand (Activating the Need):** 
  > *(Steps forward, pushing her hood back completely and locking eyes with them)* *"No. I'm not staying behind this time. I am a part of this fight now, and I'm standing by you."*
