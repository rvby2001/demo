# Master Visual Reference Sheet: Protagonist (Player Avatar)

This document merges the narrative core of the character-design profile for the **Protagonist** with standard visual prompt engineering blocks to create a single production-ready master reference sheet for visual novel artists and image generators.

To preserve the self-insert and POV style, the protagonist is visually represented as an atmospheric, shaded silhouette/shadow figure with obscured features.

---

## 1. Core Identity & Narrative Engine

### Narrative Core
* **Role in Story:** Female Protagonist / Player Avatar (POV style).
* **High-Concept Vibe:** *"The ultimate blank-slate protagonist: an adaptable, observant newcomer whose core identity, beliefs, and emotional connections are defined entirely by player choices."*
* **Signature Color Palette:**
  * **Soft Beige (#F5F5DC):** Representing the neutral tone of her trench coat, serving as a clean visual anchor.
  * **Charcoal (#36454F):** Representing the deep, moody shadows that obscure her face and body outline.
  * **Muted Ivory (#FDF5E6) [Accent]:** Representing the pages of her blank journal, where the player's choices write the fate of the world.

### The Want vs. Need Friction
* **The "Want" (External Goal):** To safely navigate her new supernatural surroundings and survive conflicts between powerful factions while keeping her options open.
  * *Details:* She acts as a passive observer, trying to remain neutral and agreeable to everyone to minimize danger.
* **The "Need" (Internal Growth):** To step out of passive observation, take a definitive stand, and commit to her choices and companions rather than trying to please everyone or remain an invisible bystander.
  * *Details:* She must understand that absolute neutrality is an illusion that leads to tragedy. True relationships and resolution require taking a side and asserting her voice.
* **The Choice Friction:**
  Represented by the main branching choice pathways of the game where she must make a definitive choice between Silas's sentinel path, Dorian's coven path, or Hercules's celestial path.
  * **Path of the Bystander (Catering to the Want):** She remains completely passive, failing to make a choice. Factions destroy each other, and she is left wandering alone in a ruined city, her journal completely blank, unable to rewrite the history she failed to shape.
  * **Path of the Author (Catering to the Need):** She takes a stand and commits to a path. She is seen standing confidently alongside her chosen companion, her hood completely down, exposing her face to the sunlight, writing the final, hopeful words in her journal with a genuine smile.

## 2. Sprite & Visual Design System

### Visual Silhouette & Distinct Features
* **The Outline Anchor:** A soft, neutral cream trench coat with an oversized hood that is often pulled up, partially casting a shadow over her eyes. This allows players of all hair styles to easily insert themselves into her role.
* **Key Accessories:**
  * *The Blank Leather Journal:* A simple, unadorned leather sketchbook where she writes her observations of the world, serving as the in-game diary/menu interface.

### Behavioral Quirks & Body Language
* **Quirk 1 (Observation Mode):** Whenever she feels overwhelmed in a group conversation, she silently opens her leather journal and starts writing notes or sketching, shifting attention away from herself.
* **Quirk 2 (The Hood Shield):** She pulls the edges of her oversized hood forward when she is embarrassed or trying to hide her reactions from others.
* **Quirk 3 (Pencil Tap):** She taps her pencil rhythmically against her chin when faced with an important decision, a physical cue for player choice prompts.

## 3. The Prompt Anatomy Formula

To generate a consistent visual novel sprite, compile the three modular blocks using the following syntax:

```text
[STYLE CANVAS] [CHARACTER IDENTITY ANCHOR], [EXPRESSION & POSE LAYER], [PRODUCTION SUFFIX]
```

### Module A: The Static Style Canvas
> *"Visual novel sprite, high-end 2D anime style, clean crisp line art, beautiful soft cell-shading, moody atmospheric lighting,"*

### Module B: The Static Character Identity Anchor
> *"...a beautiful mysterious female self-insert protagonist, represented as a shaded silhouette and shadowy figure, her eyes and facial features cast in dark shadow and obscured under an oversized hood, soft generic female silhouette, slender build, wearing a loose-fitting weathered cream trench coat with the oversized hood pulled up casting a deep shadow over the upper half of her face, holding a simple blank dark leather journal in her hands, her silhouette rendered in a soft charcoal-gray gradient and beige-tinted shadow art style..."*

### Module C: The Static Production Suffix
> *"...waist-up portrait, facing viewer, centered composition, isolated on a solid flat medium-gray background, clean edges, studio light, professional illustration, masterpiece --no 3d, photorealism, background elements, patterns, drop shadows"*

* **Good Ending Fate ("The Author of Fate"):**

## 6. Dialogue Samples

* **Generic / Observation:**
  > *"I'm just... trying to understand how this world works. Tell me more about what you do."*
* **Passive / Neutral (Default POV):**
  > *(Quietly pulls her hood down a bit, opening her journal)* *"I don't want to get between you two. Let's just find a way that keeps everyone safe."*
* **Assertive / Taking a Stand (Activating the Need):**
  > *(Steps forward, pushing her hood back completely and locking eyes with them)* *"No. I'm not staying behind this time. I am a part of this fight now, and I'm standing by you."*
