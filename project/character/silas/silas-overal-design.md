# Master Visual Reference Sheet: Silas Cross

This document merges the narrative core of the character-design profile for **Silas Cross** with standard visual prompt engineering blocks to create a single production-ready master reference sheet for visual novel artists and image generators.

---

## 1. Core Identity & Narrative Engine

### Narrative Core
* **Role in Story:** Major Love Interest / Deuteragonist / Protector Companion (a stoic, grounded foil to Dorian's theatrical vanity).
* **High-Concept Vibe:** *"The quiet, rugged vampire hunter who acts tough and distant but holds an intensely protective, deeply caring heart, communicating his devotion entirely through acts of service and protection rather than words."*
* **Signature Color Palette:**
  * **Steel Gray (#708090):** Reflects his stoicism, weapon materials, and the walls he builds around himself.
  * **Forest Green (#2E8B57):** Grounded, dark, organic; represented by his signature heavy duster coat.
  * **Weathered Silver (#C0C0C0) [Accent]:** Representing the silver weapons he fights with and the heavy medallion around his neck.

### The Want vs. Need Friction
* **The "Want" (External Goal):** To systematically eradicate the local *Nightshade Coven* (who killed his mentor) and keep the protagonist isolated in a remote "safe zone" away from all supernatural danger.
  * *Details:* Silas believes love and attachment are liabilities in a hunter's life. He wants to finish his crusade alone and place the protagonist in an isolated sanctuary where they will never be targeted.
* **The "Need" (Internal Growth):** To realize that self-sacrificing isolation is a trauma response rather than strength, and to trust the protagonist enough to let them stand beside him as an equal partner.
  * *Details:* Silas needs to learn that he doesn't have to carry the weight of the world alone. He must accept that letting someone care for him is not a weakness.
* **The Choice Friction:** 
  During Act 3, the Nightshade Coven launches a surprise raid on a nearby sanctuary town where Silas's allies are stationed.
  * **Path of the Lone Wolf (Catering to the Want):** The player stays behind in Silas's hidden bunker while he goes alone to fight. Silas successfully saves his allies but returns severely injured, emotionally colder, and locked in his belief that he must suffer alone.
  * **Path of the Partner (Catering to the Need):** The player refuses to stay behind, insisting on fighting beside him. This forces Silas to trust the player's training and face the terror of seeing someone he loves in danger. While they barely survive, Silas is forced to rely on the player to tend his wounds, letting him experience true companionship and start opening his heart.

## 2. Sprite & Visual Design System

### Visual Silhouette & Distinct Features
* **The Outline Anchor:** A heavy, floor-length weathered canvas duster coat in deep forest green, worn over a high-collared charcoal undershirt. Double leather harness straps run diagonally across his chest, holding three silver hunting stakes. His silhouette is solid, broad-shouldered, and immovable.
* **Key Accessories:**
  * *The Wolf Pendant:* A heavy, scratched silver medallion depicting a wolf biting a broken key—the emblem of his late mentor's guild. He holds or squeezes it when stressed.
  * *The Arm Wraps:* Tight, dark gray leather straps wrapped around his right forearm, concealing a jagged set of claw scars from his first encounter with an elder vampire.
  * *The Leather Utility Belt:* A rugged belt holding silver vials, a utility knife, and a heavy-duty grapple hook at his hip.

### Behavioral Quirks & Body Language
* **Quirk 1 (Silent Care):** Rather than saying "Are you alright?" or "I care about you," he will step close to the player, silently inspect their weapon straps, adjust a loose cloak buckle, or hand them a warm canteen of tea, before stepping back without a word.
* **Quirk 2 (Tension Release):** When he is hiding an injury or feeling emotionally overwhelmed, he will silently roll his right shoulder back and tighten his grip on his silver wolf pendant until his knuckles turn white.
* **Quirk 3 (The Shield):** In any transition to a new location or conversation, his sprite is positioned slightly ahead of the protagonist's, standing between them and the darkest corner of the background, his hand resting instinctively on his harness straps.

## 5. Writer's Notes & Dynamic Ending Transformations

* **Bad Ending Fate ("The Iron Sentinel"):** 
  If the player constantly permits Silas to shut them out, he becomes entirely consumed by his duty. He successfully eradicates the coven but loses his left eye in the final raid. In the epilogue, he is seen in a dark, isolated mountain cabin, standing guard at the window. He never smiles, never speaks, and wears a heavy, scar-torn black coat. He has successfully kept the player safe, but at the cost of both of their souls.
* **Good Ending Fate ("A Shared Hearth"):** 
  If the player coaxes him out of his shell, Silas eventually hangs up his hunter's coat. In the epilogue, he is seen without his duster, wearing a soft gray wool sweater with rolled-up sleeves. He stands in the doorway of a partially rebuilt country cottage in the morning sun, holding a mug of tea. He has a soft, genuine smile on his face, looking back at the player with complete, peaceful warmth.

## 6. Dialogue Samples

* **Default / Quiet:** 
  > *"Hostiles ahead. Stay behind me. Don't move until I tell you to."*
* **Exasperated / Grumpy (Action over Words):**
  > *(Grunts, stepping closer and silently pulling the protagonist's hood up over their head)* *"Wind is picking up. You'll freeze. Keep it on."*
* **Deflecting / Awkward:**
  > *(Looks away, hand holding his wolf pendant)* *"I don't need a thank you. Just... wanted to make sure you made it back. That's all. Go inside."*
* **Vulnerable / Deep Affection:**
  > *(Stares at the ground, then slowly locks his eyes onto the protagonist, his voice dropping to a whisper)* *"I spent my whole life waiting for the next fight. Running into the dark, thinking it was the only way to keep my promises. But... I don't want to run anymore. I want to stay. With you. If... you'll have me."*
