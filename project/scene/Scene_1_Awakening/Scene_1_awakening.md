# Scene Design: Scene_1_Awakening

## 1. Setting & Atmosphere
* **Background Reference:** `shadow_clearing`
* **Sensory Details:** Pale silver moonlight filtering through gnarled, shadowy tree roots; cold, damp virtual mist clinging to the ground; the harsh metallic resonance of clashing blades; the faint smell of ozone, damp moss, and ancient blood rose; the rustling of dry leaves underfoot.
* **Overall Mood:** Sudden shock, high-stakes tension, and dangerous rivalry.

---

## 2. Stage Setup & Casting
* **Left Position:** `dorian` (Active - elegant, teasing, lethal; wearing his sweeping high-collared crimson velvet tailcoat)
* **Right Position:** `silas` (Active - rugged, broad-shouldered, combative; wearing his forest green weathered canvas duster)
* **Center / Intervening:** `hercules` (Intervening - bubbly, protective, bright; his massive white wings flared wide to block their blades)
* **The Core Beat:** The player (female POV protagonist) awakens in a dangerous, unfamiliar boundary realm in the middle of a lethal fight between a vampire lord and a hunter, which is broken up by an angel.

---

## 3. Character Friction (Want vs. Need Clashes)
How do the characters' underlying motivations clash in this specific scene?

* **Silas Cross Want vs. Need:**
  * *Want in this scene:* To immediately execute Dorian (his sworn enemy) and safely lock the protagonist away in a hidden bunker away from coven reach.
  * *Need in this scene:* To accept that he cannot solve this multi-faction supernatural crisis alone and must rely on a temporary alliance rather than carrying all danger himself.
* **Dorian Vane Want vs. Need:**
  * *Want in this scene:* To defeat Silas, mock his hunting order, and charm or leverage the newly arrived human (the protagonist) to aid his own search for the Reliquary.
  * *Need in this scene:* To drop his theatrical, mocking mask and admit the danger of the moment, accepting the human's presence as a genuine vulnerability rather than a tool to manipulate.
* **Hercules Want vs. Need:**
  * *Want in this scene:* To force a happy, peaceful resolution immediately and keep the newly awakened protagonist laughing and safe.
  * *Need in this scene:* To step out of his cheerful, defensive facade and use genuine authority to command the situation rather than just hiding his own anxiety behind smiles.
* **Protagonist Want vs. Need:**
  * *Want in this scene:* To shrink away from notice, avoid getting caught between these terrifying figures, and slip back home passively.
  * *Need in this scene:* To push past her panic, take a definitive stand, and actively assert her presence to influence these powerful forces.

---

## 4. Narrative Pacing & Choice Flow
Outline the structural beats of the scene.

### Progression Beats
* **Beat 1: The Inciting Beat (Awakening):** The screen fades in from black, blurry at first, then sharpening to a cold, misty forest clearing. Gnarled tree roots and heavy shadows surround the player. She has no idea where she is and realizes she is in a world she doesn't belong to.
* **Beat 2: The Rising Tension (The Duel):** Metallic clashing rings out. A tall gentleman in a sweeping crimson-lined velvet tailcoat (Dorian) dances backward, dodging a massive forest-green canvas coat (Silas) who thrusts three silver stakes forward. Their exchange of words is venomous. Silas snarls that Dorian should feel lucky he hasn't finished him off yet. Dorian smirks lazily, adjusting his gloves, and mocks Silas's rigid hunting protocols.
* **Beat 3: The Climax / Decision Point (The Intervention):** The blades cross with a harsh screech. Silas prepares a lethal stake thrust; Dorian's crimson eyes glow predator-red as his fangs bare. Before they strike, golden feathers flutter. Hercules bursts between them, his bright white wings flared wide. He playfully but firmly blocks their paths, shouting at them to stop and pointing toward the protagonist, who has sat up.
* **Beat 4: The Truce:** Silas and Dorian freeze, their weapons inches from Hercules's feathers. They slowly lower their weapons, reluctantly agreeing to deal with their previous hatred and matters later, turning their full focus onto the protagonist.

## 5. Branching Choice Matrix
Define the choices, button texts, and dialogue results.

* **Choice 1: Passive/Fearful Retreat (Feeds the Want)**
  * *Button Text:* "Shrink back and pull your hood up."
  - *Narrative Effect:* The protagonist retreats into herself, pulling her beige hood forward. Silas steps forward protectively, placing his duster coat between her and Dorian. Dorian chuckles dryly, calling her a "shy little bird," while Hercules flies to her side, offering a reassuring smile.
  - *Psychological Value:* Feeds the Protagonist's *Want* (avoiding notice/passive survival) and Silas's *Want* (being the lone protector), but delays the protagonist's *Need* to take a stand.
  - *Routing:* Branch & Merge back to **Beat 5 (The Truce Discussions)**.

* **Choice 2: Decisive Assertion (Fosters the Need)**
  * *Button Text:* "Stand up and demand to know where you are."
  - *Narrative Effect:* The protagonist pushes herself up, brushing off leaves, and demands answers. Her boldness catches them off guard. Dorian's eyes widen in genuine shock, arching an eyebrow in amusement. Silas frowns in stoic surprise, and Hercules claps his hands with cheerful delight, praising her spirit.
  - *Psychological Value:* Fosters the Protagonist's *Need* (taking a stand, active agency) and forces the combatants to view her as an active player, not a victim.
  - *Routing:* Branch & Merge back to **Beat 5 (The Truce Discussions)**.
